Extremely Important

LNC Dual Enrollment Policy

This policy ensures that students understand the commitment required when enrolling in college-level coursework. It maintains the integrity of high school and college transcripts, prevents gaps in course schedules, and reinforces the expectation that all students are capable of meeting the academic challenges presented.

Transcripts & Dropped Courses

  • Any CPCC course a student registers for will be placed on both the LNC and college transcripts.
  • Any student who withdraws from a CPCC course or is dropped for any reason without replacing it with a new course in the same semester will receive an "F" on their high school transcript.
  • Courses may not be dropped due to difficulty, as all courses offered are college-level and inherently rigorous. Students are expected to remain enrolled for the duration.

Course Load Requirements

Students must register for at least half or more of their total CPCC courses in the first semester and may complete the remaining courses in the second semester.

Check-Ins & Drop Protocol

  • Require quarterly check-ins and progress updates to monitor progress.
  • Must fill out a change request form and meet with HS Counselor before dropping any courses.

Exceptions

A medical or compassionate withdrawal approved by CPCC is the only exception. You must notify your counselor before the class ends. The appeal must be completed and approved before the end of the grading period, or your "W" will remain an "F" on LNC’s transcript. Once documentation is provided from CPCC, your LNC transcript will be updated.
